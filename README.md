# Rojo Tree Service & Landscaping Design — private preview

A screenshot-first concept site for Rojo Tree Service & Landscaping Design in League City, Texas.

## Creative direction

**ROOTED TRANSFORMATIONS** — a mature, editorial outdoor-property identity built around canopy, root rings, topographic forms, warm limestone, deep forest green, and restrained clay accents. The site is intentionally different from a generic lawn-care or contractor template.

The three primary screenshot moments are:
1. Desktop hero — `Your property has more potential.`
2. Before/after transformation composition — `The difference should be easy to see.`
3. Mobile hero — brand + hero art + estimate CTA at ~390px.

## Deliberately not claimed

The preview does **not** claim licensed/insured/certified status, years in business, emergency or storm service, free estimates, guarantees, warranties, prices, crew size, equipment, turnaround times, or a specific service radius.

## Contact / estimate flow

Texting was not independently verified as a customer channel, so the preview does not manufacture an SMS flow. The interactive project planner builds a short project brief in-browser, lets the visitor copy it, and then offers a direct call to the published number. No form data is sent or stored.

## Photo strategy

All major visual areas are original CSS concept art or explicit `ROJO PHOTO SLOT` placeholders. Do not scrape Google/Facebook/customer uploads. After owner permission, replace the hero art and gallery placeholders with actual Rojo work:

- Hero: strongest wide transformation / mature tree / finished property
- Transformation: true before + after pair
- Gallery 01: tree transformation
- Gallery 02: landscape redesign
- Gallery 03: paver / hardscape detail
- Gallery 04: finished-property wide shot
